The problem

  • I thought I was missing information.

    I bought ebook after ebook and kept searching online. It turned out I had too much information.

  • I thought I was missing strength.

    I came from the gym and did 1 year of weighted dips and pull-ups. Even so, I spent about 2 years without a single skill.

  • I thought the next method was the one.

    I went through about 10 coaches looking for a secret method. I jumped from coach to coach.

I wasn't missing information. It was impatience disguised as wanting to learn.

Who I am

João Gonzalez, @ganzalez666

Everything changed when I was coached by an athlete I admire. I learned to train in combos, to train by feeling (the routine adapts to me, not me to the routine) and to visualise every movement. And I learned to be patient. Today I do the full planche, full front lever and freestanding handstand, and that's the method I teach.

Who it's for

For people who have trained for years and are stuck (plateau) on one of these skills:

  • Planche
  • Front lever
  • Handstand

If you've already tried everything (programs, ebooks, coaches) and you're still in the same place, I've been there.

It's not for you if…

  • You're just starting out.
  • You want quick results.
  • You switch methods every week and don't want to stop.
