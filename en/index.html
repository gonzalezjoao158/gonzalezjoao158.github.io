    Feedback

    FAQ

    Do I need equipment? Can I train at home?

    You don't need a gym. The plan is built around what you have: a full gym, a pull-up bar, dip bars, resistance bands or just your bodyweight, at home or in a park. For some calisthenics skills a bar helps a lot — if that's the case, I'll tell you the minimum you need.

    How much time do I need each week?

    It depends on your goal, but usually 3 to 5 sessions of 45 to 75 minutes. Training consistently matters more than training a lot — the plan fits the time you really have.

    How do I send the videos?

    Privately, on WhatsApp. At the start you send me videos of the movements I ask for, so I can analyse your starting point and we can set your first goals. Then, during the coaching, you film parts of your sessions and I review them 3 times a week. Videos are only used for analysis and are never shared.

    Is there a discount for longer plans?

    Yes: 3- and 6-month plans are discounted (you'll find the prices in the pricing section). We agree on the duration in our WhatsApp chat.

    What language is the coaching in?

    Portuguese and English — you choose in the form.

    I'm under 18. Can I apply?

    Yes, as long as you have permission from your parents or legal guardian. The form asks you to confirm this.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. You pay monthly and there's no lock-in: if you want to stop, just let me know before the next month.

    What if I'm not happy in the first week?

    The monthly plan comes with a guarantee: if you're not happy within the first 7 days after receiving your plan, you can stop and I'll refund what you paid. Just tell me on WhatsApp. The 3- and 6-month plans are already discounted and don't include this guarantee. On any plan you have 14 days to cancel, paying only for the time already used.

    Are results guaranteed?

    I don't promise guaranteed results — they depend on your consistency, sleep, nutrition and starting point. What I do guarantee is a structured plan, close support and adjustments whenever they're needed.
