How it works

It starts with a call. If we're a good fit, the coaching follows these steps:

  1. 30-minute call

    We talk about where you are, what has been holding you back and what you want to achieve. At the end, if we're a good fit, I explain how the coaching works.

  2. Kick-off video call

    Once you're in, we have a video call to assess you live, look at your movements and fix your technique.

  3. Personalised plan

    You get your training plan within 1 to 2 working days after the video call. Your coaching only starts counting from that moment.

  4. Reviews 3 times a week

    You send videos of your sessions on WhatsApp and I review them 3 times a week, with corrections and plan adjustments.

FAQ

Do I need equipment? Can I train at home?

You don't need a gym. The plan is built around what you have: a full gym, a pull-up bar, dip bars, resistance bands or just your bodyweight, at home or in a park. For some calisthenics skills a bar helps a lot — if that's the case, I'll tell you the minimum you need.

How much time do I need each week?

It depends on your goal, but usually 3 to 5 sessions of 45 to 75 minutes. Training consistently matters more than training a lot — the plan fits the time you really have.

How do I send the videos?

Privately, on WhatsApp. At the start you send me videos of the movements I ask for, so I can analyse your starting point and we can set your first goals. Then, during the coaching, you film parts of your sessions and I review them 3 times a week. Videos are only used for analysis and are never shared.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. We go through the price at the end of the call.

What language is the coaching in?

Portuguese and English. Tell me on the call which one you prefer.

I'm under 18. Can I book the call?

Yes, as long as you have permission from your parents or legal guardian, and one of them joins the call. The form asks you to confirm this.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You pay upfront for the period you choose, and at the end you only renew if you want to. On any plan you have 14 days to cancel, paying only for the time already used.

Are results guaranteed?

I don't promise guaranteed results — they depend on your consistency, sleep, nutrition and starting point. What I do guarantee is a structured plan, close support and adjustments whenever they're needed.
